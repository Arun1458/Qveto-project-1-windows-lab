Document the architecture of the Week 1 virtual lab and explain how the two virtual machines communicate over a dedicated internal virtual network.

## 6. Isolation Design

Both virtual machines are configured to use the same VirtualBox Internal Network. Bridged networking is not part of the intended design.

The lab should not be considered fully isolated until all enabled adapters, routing configuration, and relevant host/guest networking settings have been reviewed.

## 7. Validation

- Windows-to-Kali ping: Successful, 0% packet loss.
- Kali-to-Windows ping: Successful, 0% packet loss after enabling the relevant Windows inbound ICMPv4 Echo Request rule.
- Full adapter and route review: Pending confirmation.

## 9. Conclusion

The lab architecture consists of two virtual machines communicating over the Internal Network LabNet. The configured addresses and successful ping tests demonstrate bidirectional ICMP connectivity. Isolation review remains a separate verification requirement.

End of lab design.
