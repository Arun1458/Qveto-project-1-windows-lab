RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
===================

Authorization and scope
-----------------------
- Testing is limited to virtual machines and networks explicitly owned or
  authorized by the lab operator.
- Record approved systems, addresses, dates, and activities before testing.
- Do not target production, public, or third-party systems.

Safety controls
---------------
- Keep lab networks isolated from production and the public internet unless
  connectivity is specifically required and safely controlled.
- Do not use real credentials or store secrets in this project.
- Avoid destructive actions and preserve a clean snapshot for recovery.
- Stop immediately if activity affects a system outside the documented scope.

Data handling
-------------
- Collect only data required to demonstrate a configuration or result.
- Sanitize screenshots, logs, hostnames, and addresses before sharing.
- Store approved artifacts in evidence/; do not include passwords, tokens,
  private keys, or personal data.

Reporting
---------
- Record test dates, affected lab systems, observations, evidence references,
  and remediation in change-log.txt.
- Document exceptions and obtain renewed authorization before expanding scope.
